## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **1666: Amsterdam**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for exploration, investigation, witchcraft, and encounters with the Originals. It is designed around the game's day-and-night structure, spellcasting, character switching, potion management, and single-player progression.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more forgiving way to explore Amsterdam, investigate the Originals, experiment with witchcraft, and experience the story without repeated combat or resource-management setbacks.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Noa explores Amsterdam from a third-person perspective using standard movement, sprinting, jumping, crouching, and interaction controls. Use the movement options only when needed, especially during traversal or stealth-focused investigation. Aaron can be selected to scout areas from a different perspective before returning to Noa.

### Tools & Inventory

The game includes an inventory and potion-belt interface for managing usable items and preparing for supernatural encounters. Activate unlimited potion support before consuming items, and avoid changing inventory-related options while menus are opening, closing, or transferring items.

### World & Environment

The game alternates between daytime exploration and nighttime investigation. During the day, examine Amsterdam, search for clues, and identify citizens who may be Originals. When the Esbat begins, marked targets must be confronted in their true forms. Trainer options can make preparation and exploration less restrictive while leaving the investigation structure intact.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat centers on Noa's witchcraft, spellcasting, defensive abilities, timing, and tactical encounters with the Originals. Use resource support for extended spell testing, defensive protection for difficult encounters, and enemy-control options when learning attack patterns. One-hit defeat is best reserved for cleanup or repeated attempts after the encounter has already been experienced normally.
